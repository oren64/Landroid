FLIGHT & LANDING
Use the JOYSTICK to apply thrust. Approach planets slowly and legs down to touch down safely. Land near planet markers to enter facilities.
PLANET MARKERS
BLUE: Trade Hub (Jobs/Market)
GREEN: Shipyard (Upgrades)
PURPLE: Anomaly (Salvage)
WHITE: Mission Objective
RESOURCES & FUEL
FUEL: Siphon from Gas Giants or buy at Hubs. RESOURCES: Mine terrestre planets. OXYGEN tank is refilled when landing on a blue planet that have Oxygen. Upgrading Fuel or O2 tanks at a SHIPYARD will REFILL them to 100% instantly. WRAP QUARTZ: Required for interstellar travel.
SURVIVAL ADVICE
Upgrade your WEAPONS and SHIELD early to survive pirate ambushes. Upgrade your Fuel or O2 tanks to fly longer and survive long battles.
